COMPANY POLICY HANDBOOK (SAMPLE)

1. Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
All remote connections must use company VPN. Remote work requests must be
submitted via the HR portal at least 5 business days in advance.

2. Security Training
All employees must complete annual security awareness training by January 31st.
New hires must complete training within their first 30 days. Failure to
complete training results in automatic account suspension until completed.

3. Incident Response
Any suspected security incident must be reported to the SecOps team within
1 hour of discovery via the #incident-response Slack channel or the
emergency hotline. Do not attempt to remediate a suspected breach yourself.

4. Expense Reimbursement
Business expenses under $50 do not require pre-approval. Expenses over $50
require manager sign-off before purchase. Submit reimbursement requests
within 30 days of the expense date via the Finance portal.

5. Data Retention
Customer data is retained for 7 years per regulatory requirement, then
automatically purged. Internal logs are retained for 90 days unless subject
to a legal hold.

6. Password Policy
Passwords must be at least 14 characters, rotated every 90 days, and must
not be reused across the last 10 passwords. MFA is mandatory for all
systems containing customer data.
